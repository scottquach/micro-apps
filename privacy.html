Privacy

Tool inputs and outputs stay in your browser. Do not send sensitive content through these utilities unless you are comfortable handling it on your own device.

Analytics

This project can use PostHog to understand which tools are opened and whether common UI actions work. Analytics are disabled in the open-source config until a deployment owner enables them.

  • Captured: page path, document title, app name, and explicit usage events.
  • Not captured: pasted text, uploaded files, generated output, JSON payloads, Base64 values, or color inputs.
  • Session replay and automatic click/input capture are disabled.
